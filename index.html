RemindThat

提提我用来记下证件、药品、日用品的到期日，以及要做的事，并在这台 iPhone 上提醒你。不需要注册账号。记录、照片和设置只保存在手机里。

支持 iOS 16.4 及以上。简体中文界面显示「提提我」；香港、澳门及粤语系统显示「提下你」；其他语言显示 RemindThat。

自己先看这几项

  • 提醒没出现：到系统设置里确认已允许「提提我」通知，并看是否开了勿扰或专注模式。打开应用时会安排接下来的提醒。长时间不打开，更后面的日期不一定已经排上。同一时间的几条提醒会合成一条。
  • 日期或名称不对：识别结果要在确认页核对后才会保存。可以直接改字。只写了年月的日期会按当月最后一天处理，并提示你核对。
  • 语音：可以选择普通话、粤语或 English。默认在本机识别。在「设置」里关闭「离线识别」，或者这台手机不支持所选语言的离线识别时，语音会发给 Apple。录音不保留，只保存转写文字。
  • 换手机：在「设置」的「备份与恢复」里导出备份，再到新手机上恢复。备份包含记录、照片、分类和提醒设置，不包含 AI 的 API Key。文件带完整性校验，正文是明文，不是加密文件。恢复会替换手机里现有的资料；恢复前的资料可以在设置里取回。
  • 删掉资料：在应用里删除对应记录，或直接卸载。卸载后，手机里的记录和照片会一起删除。应用资料不参与 iCloud 设备备份。

Support

提提我 · RemindThat

RemindThat keeps expiry dates for documents, medicine, and everyday items, and reminders for things you need to do, on this iPhone. There is no account. Records, photos, and settings stay on the phone.

It requires iOS 16.4 or later. The name is 提提我 in Simplified Chinese, 提下你 on Hong Kong, Macau, and Cantonese systems, and RemindThat in other languages.

Check these first

  • A reminder did not appear: allow notifications for 提提我, and check Focus or Do Not Disturb. Opening the app schedules the upcoming reminders. Dates further out may not be scheduled until you open it again. Several reminders at the same time are combined into one notification.
  • A date or name looks wrong: nothing is saved until you confirm the page. You can edit the fields. A year-and-month date is taken as the last day of that month, and the app asks you to check it.
  • Speech: you can use Mandarin, Cantonese, or English. Recognition stays on the phone by default. If you turn off “On-device recognition” in Settings, or this iPhone cannot recognize the selected language on device, the audio is sent to Apple. Recordings are not kept. Only the transcript is saved.
  • A new phone: export a backup from Settings, under “Backup & restore”, then restore it on the new phone. The file includes records, photos, categories, and reminder settings. It does not include an AI API key. It has an integrity check and is stored as readable data, not as an encrypted archive. Restoring replaces what is on the phone. The previous data can be brought back from Settings.
  • Removing data: delete the record in the app, or uninstall it. Uninstalling deletes the records and photos stored by the app. The app’s private data is excluded from iCloud device backup.

Contact

Open a note on GitHub Issues. Do not attach an API key, document photos, medicine photos, or a backup file.
